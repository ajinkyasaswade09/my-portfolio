About

From PRD to Play Store, end to end

I'm a results-driven Android engineer who has spent 12 years taking mobile products from concept to launch — across banking, fintech, media & entertainment, B2B, B2C, healthcare and travel. I care about clean, modular architecture, honest test coverage, and user experiences that feel effortless.

At Intuit — where I work as a contingent worker via Magnit Global — I'm on the Business Credit Card app: building agentic AI workflows for Android development, adding voice-command navigation, and running an end-to-end pipeline that turns PRDs into HTML prototypes, syncs designs with Figma, and lands production-ready iOS/Android features.

Before that I spent eight years at Globant building platform libraries for HSBC and Goldman Sachs — plugin systems that scaled to multiple countries, a security library with root detection and a custom secure keypad, and an app revamp that touched 2 million users. Along the way: Jetpack Compose on Android TV, Roku, Flutter, and a healthcare app that got acquired.
